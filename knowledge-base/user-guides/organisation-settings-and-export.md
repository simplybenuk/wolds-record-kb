# Organisation settings and data export

## Organisation settings

Open **Organisation settings** to review and maintain core details for your active organisation, including contact and branding-related fields used across the system.

Use **Edit details** on the **Organisation details** card to update contact details, the timezone, the default session form and vet report branding.

## Beta access

Owners and admins can switch beta access on or off from the **Beta access** card. The change saves straight away and applies to everyone in the organisation. Turning it off asks for confirmation first, because Calendar and public booking links stop working until it is turned back on. Booking data is kept. See [Calendar (beta)](calendar.md) for full diary and booking guidance.

## Billing

The **Billing** card shows your organisation's current plan, billing status and monthly session usage.

Only owners and admins can start or manage subscriptions. Members see the plan details and are asked to contact an owner or admin to change the plan.

### Plans and upgrades

- **Free**: includes standard session limits. Owners and admins can select **Upgrade to Starter** (or **Start first month free** if eligible) or **Choose Pro monthly**.
- **Starter**: includes unlimited sessions. Owners and admins can select **Choose Pro monthly** or **Manage billing**.
- **Pro**: includes unlimited sessions and premium features. Owners and admins can select **Manage billing**.

Subscriptions renew automatically each month or year until cancelled.

### Manage or cancel a subscription

To update payment details or cancel a subscription, select **Manage billing** on the **Billing** card to open the Stripe customer portal. Cancel before your next billing date to avoid renewal.

## Export organisation data

Organisation settings includes export actions.

### Export formats

- **JSON**
- **CSV ZIP**

### Export flow

1. Choose your export format.
2. Start export.
3. Download begins from a time-limited secure link.

### Export contents

Exports include:

- Clients
- Dogs
- Cases
- Sessions

If export cannot start, an error message is shown and you can retry.
