# Organisation settings and data export

## Organisation settings

Open **Organisation settings** to review and maintain core details for your active organisation, including contact and branding-related fields used across the system.

Use **Edit details** on the **Organisation details** card to update contact details, the timezone, the default session form and vet report branding.

Only owners and admins can edit organisation details and terms and privacy.
Other members see a **Read only** badge.

### Services covered by consent forms

**Services covered by consent forms** describes your services, for example
physiotherapy, hydrotherapy or canine massage. New owner and veterinary consent
forms use this wording. Leave it blank to keep the standard wording.

1. Select **Edit details**.
2. Enter your services in **Services covered by consent forms**.
3. Select **Save changes**.

### Terms and privacy

The **Terms and privacy** card holds two optional entries:

- **Information and terms**: appointment information and terms of service,
  shown on booking pages and owner consent forms.
- **Privacy notice**: your organisation's privacy information, shown on booking
  pages, owner consent forms and vet declaration forms.

To change them:

1. Select **Edit terms and privacy**.
2. For each entry, choose **Not provided**, **Enter text** or **Provide a
   link**.
3. For **Enter text**, write the text. The toolbar offers headings, bold,
   italic, bulleted and numbered lists, and links.
4. For **Provide a link**, enter a complete web address starting with
   `https://` or `http://`.
5. Select **Save changes**.

Changes apply to current booking pages and to consent forms issued after you
save. A consent form keeps the terms and privacy notice that applied when its
link was issued, and a signed form keeps the wording the signer saw.

## Beta access

Owners and admins can switch beta access on or off from the **Beta access** card. The change saves straight away and applies to everyone in the organisation. Turning it off asks for confirmation first, because Calendar and public booking links stop working until it is turned back on. Booking data is kept.

## Billing

The **Billing** card shows your current plan, billing status and session usage.
Owners and admins can upgrade from it.

Each plan shows its price and renewal terms before you go to checkout.
Subscriptions renew automatically every month or year until cancelled. Eligible
organisations get the first month of a monthly Starter plan free, after which
the monthly price applies.

To cancel, select **Manage billing** on the **Billing** card before your next
billing date.

## Export organisation data

Organisation settings includes export actions.

### Export formats

- **JSON**
- **CSV ZIP**

### Export flow

1. Choose your export format.
2. Start export.
3. Download begins from a time-limited secure link.

### Export contents

Exports include:

- Clients
- Dogs
- Cases
- Sessions

If export cannot start, an error message is shown and you can retry.
