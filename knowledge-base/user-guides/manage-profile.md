# Manage your account

Your account has two pages, both in the **Account** menu in the header:

- **My Account**: your contact details.
- **Personal settings**: your appearance and session preferences.

## My Account

Use **My Account** to keep your contact details current.

### Update contact details

1. Open the **Account** menu and select **My Account**.
2. Select **Edit profile**.
3. Edit your **Full name**.
4. Select **Save profile**.

If successful, you will see **Profile updated**.

Keep your full name accurate for reports and team visibility.

## Personal settings

**Personal settings** apply to your account across organisations and devices.

### Choose light or dark mode

1. Open the **Account** menu and select **Personal settings**.
2. Under **Appearance**, choose a **Colour mode**:
   - **System**: follows your device's light or dark appearance.
   - **Light**
   - **Dark**
3. Select **Save personal settings**.

### Choose your default session form

The default session form is the form pre-selected when you create a session.
You can still change the form for each session.

1. Open the **Account** menu and select **Personal settings**.
2. Under **Session defaults**, choose a **Default session form**.
3. Select **Save personal settings**.
