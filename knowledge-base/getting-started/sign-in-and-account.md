# Sign in and account setup

## Create an account

1. Open the sign-in screen.
2. Leave the mode set to **Create your account**.
3. Enter your **Email address** and **Password**.
4. Tick **I agree to the Terms and Conditions.** Use **Read the Terms and
   Conditions** to open them first. You cannot create an account without
   agreeing.
5. Select **Create account**.
6. Complete the bot protection check if prompted.
7. Open the verification email and confirm the account before signing in.

## Sign in with an existing account

1. On the sign-in screen, select **Use existing account**.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Complete the bot protection check if prompted.
4. Select **Sign in**.

After success, you are redirected to your dashboard.

## Agree to updated Terms and Conditions

When the Terms and Conditions change, or if you have not yet agreed to the
current version, Wolds Record shows **Review the Terms and Conditions** after
you sign in. You see it once for each new version.

1. Select **Read the Terms and Conditions** to read them.
2. Tick **I agree to the Terms and Conditions.**
3. Select **Agree and continue**.

You then continue to the page you were opening. If you do not want to agree,
select **Sign out**. You cannot use your workspace until you agree.

## Sign out

Use **Sign Out** in the top navigation. This ends your current session and returns you to the sign-in page.

## Common issues

- **Invalid login credentials**: re-check email/password and try again.
- **No confirmation email**: check junk/spam and retry sign-up.
- **Email not verified**: open the latest verification email, complete
  confirmation, then sign in again.
- **Session lost**: sign in again from `/sign-in`.
- **Terms could not be loaded**: select **Try again** on the sign-up form, or
  **Reload terms** on the review screen.
- **Your agreement could not be saved**: select **Reload terms**, tick the box
  again and select **Agree and continue**.
