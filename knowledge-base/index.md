# Wolds Record help

<p class="wr-intro">Guides for organisations and practitioners using Wolds
Record to keep client, dog and treatment records.</p>

## New to Wolds Record?

Follow these steps in order:

1. [Sign in and create your account](getting-started/sign-in-and-account.md)
2. [Create or join your organisation](getting-started/organisation-onboarding.md)
3. [Set up your account](user-guides/manage-profile.md)
4. [Create your first client and dog](user-guides/clients-and-dogs.md)
5. [Open a case](user-guides/create-and-manage-cases.md) and
   [record your first session](user-guides/record-sessions.md)
